Pastoral Privacy Policy

Kingdom Ekklesias Koinonia (KEK) — Data Protection & Confidentiality Charter

1. Commitment to Confidentiality

At Kingdom Ekklesias Koinonia (KEK), we understand that submitting personal, spiritual, or corporate information involves deep trust. We maintain absolute pastoral confidentiality regarding every Prayer Dossier, Courts of Heaven petition, and partner record submitted through our digital intake portals[cite: 4].

"Your confidential prayer requests, family timelines, health dockets, and corporate goals are treated as holy trusts before the Lord and are handled exclusively by consecrated watchmen bound by strict ministerial confidentiality."

2. Information Collected

To provide targeted daily intercessory coverage under The Aaron & Hur Altar Initiative and facilitate virtual Courtroom sessions, we collect personal details including:

  • Contact Information: Legal name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp contact details, and physical country/city of residence[cite: 4].
  • Spiritual & Personal Dossiers: Family bloodline patterns, generational health challenges, marital goals, and 90-day prayer priorities[cite: 4].
  • Enterprise & Career Dockets: Corporate goals, business dockets, and financial stewardship objectives[cite: 4].

3. Usage & Access Restrictions

Information collected is used strictly for spiritual warfare, judicial petition drafting, intercessory watch matching, and administrative communications[cite: 4]:

  • Strict Watchmen Access: Your dossier is accessible only to your assigned Primary/Lead Intercessors and senior pastoral leadership[cite: 4].
  • No Commercial Exploitation: We never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to third-party advertisers or commercial entities.
  • Secure Submission Processing: Form submissions are handled securely via encrypted endpoints (`Formspree` / `Google Forms`) and stored within password-protected administrative databases[cite: 9].

4. Data Retention & Partner Rights

Partners retain full ownership over their spiritual records. You may request the update, archiving, or complete deletion of your prayer dossier at any time by contacting ministry administration directly at kingdom.ekklesias.inv@gmail.com.
